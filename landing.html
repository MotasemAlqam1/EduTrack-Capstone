Features

Everything you need to run your classes

From the first enrolment to the final exam, EduTrack keeps the important numbers and materials in one place.

  • Student management

    Add students, enrol them in your courses and keep their details up to date. Archive a student without losing their history.

  • Grades & analytics

    See the grade distribution, course averages and top performers at a glance, with no spreadsheets to maintain.

  • Early alerts

    Students who fall below the passing line are highlighted for you, so you can step in before it is too late.

  • Assessments

    Plan quizzes, assignments and exams for each course and keep every due date in view.

  • Course resources

    Search for videos and PDFs by topic and attach them to the right course, ready to share.

  • Your own space

    Every instructor signs in to a personal dashboard that shows only their own courses and students.

About us

Teaching is hard enough. Tracking it shouldn't be.

EduTrack exists so that instructors can spend their time teaching, not stitching together spreadsheets. We bring students, grades, assessments and course materials into one simple dashboard.

Our goal is a clear picture of your class at any moment: who is doing well, who needs support, and what to share next, early enough to make a difference.

How it works

  1. Create your account Register as an instructor and sign in to your dashboard.
  2. Set up your classes Add your courses and enrol your students.
  3. Track, support and share Record grades, review the analytics and attach videos and PDFs to each course.
